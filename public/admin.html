Client handoff

Follow these checks before a client launches.

Confirm payment
Open the TRONSCAN link. Check it is confirmed, successful, USDT (TRC20), for the full order amount, and sent to TRZPJcgHgm4d6v864CwAa4AogMog6RWuHY. A screenshot alone is not proof. Never ask for a wallet recovery phrase.
Connect the customer’s domain
The customer owns the domain, pays its renewals, and adds the DNS records we provide. Never ask them for their registrar password.
Set up Deriv login
The customer registers their own Deriv app, chooses their markup there, and gives Pipforge the app ID. Set the callback to https://their-domain/api/deriv/callback. Open Deriv app dashboard.
Check and hand over
Wait for DNS and SSL to become ready, open the live domain, test Deriv login, then mark the remaining setup steps done below.
