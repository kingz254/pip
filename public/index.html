Questions, answered.

Can’t find yours? Send us a message below.

Is Pipforge part of Deriv?

No. Pipforge is independent and not affiliated with Deriv. Your site links to Deriv, and Deriv’s own terms apply to your account there.

Do I keep my earnings?

Yes. We charge a setup fee and a yearly domain fee. We take no share of your trading results or referral commissions, which Deriv pays under its own partner terms.

Will the bots make me money?

We can’t promise that, and nobody honestly can. Most retail traders lose money. Test every bot on a demo account first.

Can I bring my own domain?

You choose a name and ending when you order. Tell us in your message if you want to use one you already own.

What happens when my domain renews?

The yearly domain fee is billed each year. If it isn’t paid, the site goes offline until it is.

Can I remove the risk warnings?

No. They protect you and your visitors, and every template keeps them.
